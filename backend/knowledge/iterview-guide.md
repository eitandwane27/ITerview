# ITerview student guide

## About ITerview
Keywords: what is iterview, about iterview, how does iterview work, what can iterview do, features, platform, system

ITerview helps IT students practice interview answers aloud, receive AI feedback, and review their progress. It offers role-specific technical practice and behavioral interview practice. Track suggestions are a starting point based on your interests, not an aptitude assessment or a guarantee of employment.

## Getting started
Keywords: get started, getting started, sign up, signup, register, registration, create account, login, log in, sign in

Create an account using the sign-up form, then sign in and open your dashboard. Choose your target role in the dashboard before starting the interview journey. Complete the initial confidence survey and microphone check, then take the starting check (pre-test). The chatbot's Start this track button opens registration; you still choose your role in the dashboard.

## Interview journey
Keywords: interview flow, interview journey, stages, steps, pre-test, pretest, post-test, posttest, starting check, progress check

The journey includes an initial confidence survey, microphone check, starting check (pre-test), three practice sets, progress check (post-test), final confidence survey, and results. The starting check establishes a baseline and helps identify a focus for practice. The progress check compares later answers with the baseline. These checks are normally completed once per account; use the dashboard for available practice afterward.

## Practice sets
Keywords: practice sets, set 1, set one, set 2, set two, set 3, set three, technical practice, behavioral, star

Set 1 focuses on a personalized foundation. Set 2 explores technical questions for your selected role. Set 3 practices behavioral storytelling with Situation, Action, and Result (STAR). You answer aloud and receive feedback. Use examples from your studies, projects, or teamwork when practicing behavioral answers.

## Scores and feedback
Keywords: score, scores, scoring, feedback, rubric, clarity, correctness, completeness, 3c, results, report

Clarity means how clearly you explain your answer. Correctness means whether the answer is technically accurate. Completeness means whether it covers the necessary points. Results show assessment comparisons and practice feedback, with scores displayed on a scale out of 5. Technical and behavioral sets also show their own feedback dimensions. Scores describe these practice answers, not your overall ability or employability. The public chatbot cannot retrieve your personal scores; sign in to view your dashboard and results.

## Dashboard and progress
Keywords: dashboard, progress, resume, paused, pause, difficulty, drills, focus drill, switch track, change track

The dashboard lets you configure your target role, review completed attempts and feedback, and continue an available saved session. A role may be locked while a journey is active; use the dashboard's available controls before starting fresh. Focus drills provide extra practice without changing the main journey's progress. Difficulty advancement depends on the combined practice-set average rather than the progress-check score.

## Microphone help
Keywords: microphone, mic, audio, cannot hear, can't hear, no sound, permission, recording

Allow microphone permission in your browser, select the correct input device, and complete the microphone check. Use a quiet space and check that your microphone is not muted. If permission is blocked, enable it in the browser's site settings and retry. Check your speaker volume if you cannot hear the interviewer. The chatbot cannot inspect your device or change permissions for you.

## Account help
Keywords: forgot password, reset password, password reset, account help, cannot login, can't login

Use Forgot password on the sign-in form to request a reset link for your registered email address. Follow that email to reset your password. Never send passwords or verification codes in this chat. The chatbot cannot access or change accounts, view another student's records, or perform a password reset itself.

## Assistant capabilities
Keywords: what can you do, what do you know, help me, chatbot, assistant, privacy, private data

This assistant explains the documented ITerview features and helps you choose among the current practice tracks. It cannot access student records, account details, personal results, or recordings. It cannot perform actions in your account. Avoid sharing personal or sensitive information. Questions entered here may be processed by an external AI service when conversational AI is enabled. If a feature, price, policy, or detail is not documented, the assistant should say it does not have confirmed information.
